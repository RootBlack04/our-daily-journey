A CLEAR PROMISE

Your privacy matters.

Our Daily Journey is built for a small, private circle: you and one other person you choose.

What the app stores

Your username, optional display name, timezone, the progress you choose to enter, tasks, shared notes, and the membership information needed to run your private space. Supabase Auth retains account identifiers; the administrator's existing Auth email remains inside Supabase and is not shown by this website. Newly created members do not provide an email address or phone number.

Who can see your progress

Only you and the other member of your space can read its logs, tasks, and notes. Your space member can see entries you choose to save. Logs and tasks can only be changed or deleted by their creator. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

What this app does not collect

There is no analytics, fingerprinting, location tracking, or collection of raw IP addresses in application tables. This app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, health data, GPS, or device files. Step counts and activity are manual entries; we cannot verify them.

Service providers

The app uses Supabase for authentication and database hosting, GitHub Pages for the static site, and jsDelivr to deliver the Supabase JavaScript client. These providers receive standard network requests when your browser loads or uses the app and may process connection information such as IP addresses under their own policies. The application does not write raw IP addresses to its tables. Review each provider's terms and privacy information.

Retention and deletion

Your entries remain until you or your space member removes them or your project administrator deletes the account. To delete your account, contact the person who owns the Supabase project. They can open Authentication → Users, select your account, and choose Delete user. Your profile, logs, tasks, notes, membership, username mapping, and invitations are removed. If your space member remains, they become its owner; if no member remains, the space is removed. Export anything you want to keep first.

Security

Browser configuration contains a public Supabase publishable key. It is not a secret. Database access is restricted with row level security and server-side functions. Service or secret keys stay in Supabase's server environment and are never placed in this site. Passwords are verified by Supabase Auth; the website does not store password text or custom password hashes.

Questions

This is a self-hosted project. You control its Supabase and GitHub accounts. For privacy requests, contact the project owner who configured your deployment.
